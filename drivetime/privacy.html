DriveTime — Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Welcome to DriveTime ("the App"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data of our users. This Privacy Policy explains how DriveTime collects, uses, stores, and handles your information when you use our mobile application on Android.

By downloading, installing, or using DriveTime, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect and How It Is Used

DriveTime is designed with a privacy-first, local-storage approach. The vast majority of your data is processed and stored strictly on your local device.

A. Location Data (GPS)

  • What We Access: Precise location (GPS) and background location access while a driving session is active.
  • Purpose: To track driving session durations, calculate distance, display real-time position on Google Maps, render route breadcrumbs, and record route history.
  • Storage: Location data is stored locally on your device inside the app's database. We do not sell, transfer, or upload your real-time or historical location data to any external server.

B. Camera & Video Recording (Dash Cam)

  • What We Access: Device camera(s) (front and/or rear).
  • Purpose: Provides optional dash cam recording capabilities (road view, driver cabin view, or dual view) during an active driving session.
  • Storage: All recorded video files are stored locally in your device's storage. Video files are never automatically uploaded to any cloud service or external server.

C. Microphone & Audio Recording

  • What We Access: Device microphone.
  • Purpose: Provides optional audio commentary recording during a driving session so drivers or driving instructors can leave verbal notes linked to a drive log.
  • Storage: Recorded audio files are saved locally on your device. Audio data is never transmitted to external servers.

D. Sensor Data (Accelerometer / Telemetry)

  • What We Access: Internal device motion sensors (accelerometer).
  • Purpose: Monitors lateral and longitudinal forces during an active drive to detect and log telemetry events such as hard braking, sudden acceleration, and sharp turns.
  • Storage: Telemetry event timestamps are saved locally on your device.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

DriveTime uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device for functional and advertising purposes.

Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)

DriveTime integrates Google AdMob to display banner advertisements. AdMob may collect and process certain data, including:

  • Device Advertising ID (Android AAID)
  • IP Address
  • General location data (city/country level)
  • App interaction statistics and ad performance metrics

For more details on how Google processes ad data, please review Google's Privacy Policy and Google AdMob Privacy Overview.

Google Maps SDK

DriveTime uses the Google Maps Android SDK to render interactive maps and route polylines. Use of Google Maps is subject to the Google Maps Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

4. Data Sharing & User Controls

  • No Third-Party Data Sale: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data, video recordings, audio recordings, or location logs to third parties.
  • User-Initiated Export & Sharing: DriveTime allows you to generate PDF driving logs or CSV spreadsheets for DMV submission or personal records. Exported files or shared videos are transmitted only when you explicitly choose to share them via third-party Android apps (e.g., email, messaging apps, Google Drive).
  • Data Deletion: You can delete individual drive entries, video files, or audio commentary directly within the App at any time. Uninstalling the App or clearing app data via Android Settings will permanently remove all locally stored drive history.

5. Children’s Privacy

DriveTime is intended for new drivers (typically ages 13 and older) and their supervising parents, guardians, or driving instructors. DriveTime does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal data to us, please contact us so we can take appropriate measures.
